Teams & Drivers

Explore the teams and drivers of the 2026 season.

Mercedes Ferrari McLaren Red Bull Alpine

Mercedes-AMG PETRONAS F1 Team is one of the most successful teams in Formula One history, competing at the highest level since 1954. The team has won multiple Constructors' Championships and boasts a record number of wins.

Scuderia Ferrari is the oldest and most successful team in Formula One, with a rich history dating back to 1950. The team has won numerous Constructors' and Drivers' Championships, making it a legendary name in motorsport.

McLaren Racing Limited is a British Formula One team with a storied history of success. Founded in 1963, McLaren has won multiple Constructors' and Drivers' Championships, and is known for its innovative engineering and competitive spirit.

Red Bull Racing is an Austrian Formula One team that has quickly risen to prominence since its debut in 2005. Known for its aggressive racing style and innovative car designs, Red Bull has won multiple Constructors' and Drivers' Championships, making it a formidable competitor on the track.

Alpine F1 Team is a French Formula One team that has been competing since 2021. The team is a subsidiary of the Renault Group and has quickly established itself as a competitive force in the sport, with a focus on innovation and performance.
